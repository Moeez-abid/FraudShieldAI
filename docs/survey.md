# User Validation Survey (draft)

Target: 15–20 respondents (students, staff, small-business workers). About 5 minutes.
Put it on Google Forms. Collect no names or emails; ask for an optional contact only if the person agrees to a follow-up trial.

**Intro text:** "We are building a student prototype that checks suspicious messages and links and explains why they might be a scam. Your answers are anonymous and used only for our course project."

## Section A: Experience

1. In the last 3 months, how often have you received a message you suspected was a scam?
   Never / Once or twice / Monthly / Weekly / Almost daily
2. Where did those messages arrive? (select all)
   SMS / WhatsApp / Email / Social media DM / Phone call / Other
3. What were they about? (select all)
   Bank or payment problem / Delivery / Prize or lottery / Job offer / Account verification / Government or tax / Someone pretending to be family or friend / Other
4. When you get a suspicious message today, what do you usually do?
   Ignore it / Delete it / Search online / Ask someone / Contact the organisation directly / Click to check / Other
5. Have you, or someone you know, ever lost money or account access to a scam message? Yes / No / Prefer not to say

## Section B: The product idea

6. How useful would it be to paste a message or link and get a risk rating with reasons? (1 = not useful, 5 = very useful)
7. Which would make you trust the result more? (select up to 2)
   Seeing which words or link features triggered it / A clear Low-Medium-High rating / A percentage score / Knowing how accurate it was in testing / Advice on what to do next
8. How important is it that your message is **not stored**? (1–5)
9. Where would you most want this? Website / Mobile app / WhatsApp bot / Browser extension / Built into my email

## Section C: Willingness to pay (no commitment)

10. Would you pay for an extended version (history, unlimited checks, family sharing)?
    No, free only / Maybe, under PKR 200/month / Maybe, PKR 200–500/month / Yes, more than PKR 500/month
11. (Business or organisation staff only) Would your organisation pay for an API that checks messages automatically? Yes / Maybe / No / Not applicable

## Section D: Live trial (in person, optional)

The participant tries 3 sample messages in the app, then answers:
12. Were the reasons understandable? (1–5)
13. Did any result surprise you or seem wrong? (free text)
14. Anything you would change? (free text)

## Analysis plan
- Report n, the distribution for each question, and the median usefulness and trust scores.
- Quote no more than 3 free-text answers, anonymised.
- Don't claim demand from small numbers; present results as early signals.
